CLAUDE GAMES

A growing arcade of small, complete, single-file browser games — each built to the same contract: a real menu, a pause screen, synthesized sound, and one signature twist. No installs, no assets, no build step. Pick one and play.

Gravity Runner

FLIPSIDE

Twist — the graze combo: the later you flip before a spike, the bigger the score.
Play: one button — flip gravity to dodge.
Goal: run forever, hug death, climb the multiplier.
▶ Play FLIPSIDE
 Arena Survivor

CHROMASHIFT

Twist — color polarity: your shots only hit enemies that match your current color. Flip to fight.
Play: move + flip color; shooting auto-aims.
Goal: survive escalating waves, build combos.
▶ Play CHROMASHIFT
 Asteroid Arena

GRAVLASSO

Twist — no guns: grab asteroids with your gravity lasso and fling them into enemies and each other.
Play: thrust with WASD, aim with mouse, hold Space to lasso.
Goal: survive escalating waves, chain combo multipliers.
▶ Play GRAVLASSO
 Mirror Snake

GEMINI

Twist — mirror control: you steer two snakes at once; Left/Right are flipped, so the amber twin reflects the cyan one across the seam.
Play: arrows/WASD — Up/Down move both, Left/Right mirror.
Goal: feed both twins, crash neither, chase the SYNC bonus.
▶ Play GEMINI
 Parry Arena

AEGIS

Twist — no gun: your shield is the weapon. Parry bullets to fire them straight back into the turrets that shot them.
Play: aim the shield with the mouse; Space/click for the Burst.
Goal: survive the storm, chain parries, send death back.
▶ Play AEGIS
 Time Shooter

TEMPO

Twist — time moves only when you do: stand still and the world freezes; move and the storm roars to full speed.
Play: WASD/arrows move, mouse aim, click to shoot.
Goal: clear 12 waves; freeze time, line up the shot, dance the gaps.
▶ Play TEMPO
 Echo Arena

ENCORE

Twist — every life replays beside you: die and your whole run returns as an unkillable echo that fires alongside your next self.
Play: WASD move, mouse aim, hold to fire.
Goal: stack enough past selves to burst the CORE before 7 takes run out.
▶ Play ENCORE
 Molten Breakout

EMBER

Twist — keep the ball hot: chain bricks and rally to go molten and plow through rows; let it cool and it just clinks off without breaking.
Play: mouse / touch or A D to move; Space/click to launch.
Goal: clear every brick across 5 levels — don't let it cool.
▶ Play EMBER

Each game is a self-contained index.html in its folder — open it directly in any modern browser. No server, no dependencies. Source & build contract live in GAME_AGENT.md.
